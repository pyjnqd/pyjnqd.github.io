Robot Learning Group

Research Vision

Our research group aim to solve the basic problems of Embodied AI, prioritizing the studies on real-world generalization. Anticipating the bottlenecks of future robot deployment, we study the dynamics of policy pre-training, specifically taxonomy, scaling behaviors and efficiency, in a data-centric manner. On highly capable pretrained policies, our research explores post-training paradigms for self-learning/adaptation from human and environmental feedback, enabling robots to continuously improve, and exhibit dynamic adaptation in unstructured environments.

Research Themes

Pre-training Generalization

We investigate the fundamental data dynamics required to train robust VLA foundation models, formalizing data curation as a rigorous subject of scientific inquiry. It is critical for us to explore what data recipes are optimal, how to best utilize them, and how to build a comprehensive data pipeline for VLA pre-training generalization.

Self-Learning & Adaptation

Our second focus is lifelong adaptation in the wild. Since pre-trained priors are insufficient for open-world environments, robots must continuously self-learn/adapt by extracting human or environmental feedback, which entails some non-trivial ML challenges, including sample efficiency and knowledge representation.
